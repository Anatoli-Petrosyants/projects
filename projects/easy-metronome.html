About the app

Easy Metronome keeps a steady tempo for musicians at any level. A large visual beat display makes lessons easier to follow, with up to 16 beats per bar and adjustable emphasis or silence on each.

Experienced players get quick time-signature selection, subdivisions for different playing patterns, and tap-tempo entry with decimal precision up to 999 BPM.

What I did

  • iOS development on a low-latency audio utility.
  • Precise timing engine driving the visual beat display.
  • Configurable time signatures, subdivisions and beat sounds.
