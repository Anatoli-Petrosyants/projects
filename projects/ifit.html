About the app

iFIT is a global fitness platform with over 10,000 on-demand workouts filmed in outdoor locations across all seven continents: high-intensity cardio, full-body strength, cycling, yoga, Pilates, HIIT, barre, marathon training plans, stretching, walking and mindfulness.

The app pairs with iFIT-enabled treadmills, bikes, ellipticals and rowers, automatically adjusting incline, resistance and speed to follow the terrain a trainer is running. Millions of people use it to train at home.

What I did

  • Built heart rate collection and live workout session flows on HealthKit.
  • Worked inside a codebase serving 10,000+ workouts to millions of users.
