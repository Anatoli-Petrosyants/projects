About the app

Palettes Lab is a color tool for designers, artists and photographers, anyone whose work depends on getting color exactly right. Build a brand, refine a mood board or match a paint, and every palette is one tap away.

Colors are picked from any photo or the live camera, with HEX, RGB, CMYK and harmony breakdowns for every shade. Palettes group into folders by brand, room, client or shoot, and on-device AI suggests tags as you scan, and nothing leaves the phone.

What I did

  • My own app: concept, design, development and App Store release.
  • Live camera and photo color extraction with precise picking.
  • On-device AI tagging, so nothing leaves the phone.
  • Folder organization, reordering, renaming and tag filtering.
