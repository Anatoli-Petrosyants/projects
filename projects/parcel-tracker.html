About the app

Parcel Tracker uses computer vision and OCR to make mailroom handling fast. Logging a parcel is as simple as pointing the device at the label: the tenant name and barcode are detected instantly and the tenant is notified automatically.

Collection is handled with scannable QR notifications and a captured signature as proof. Data syncs to the server for backup and stays on device for offline use.

What I did

  • iOS development on a camera-first logistics tool.
  • Label scanning with OCR and barcode detection.
  • Offline-first data layer with server sync.
